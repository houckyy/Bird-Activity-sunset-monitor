Shoot Log

Add a photo and the app reads its actual color — saturation and how much warm orange/pink tone is in it — and pre-fills the "Light quality - actually" rating below with its best guess. Use the raw, unedited photo straight off your camera or phone — filters, presets, and edited saturation/warmth change the actual pixels this reads, so an edited photo will score how it looks after editing, not how the sky really looked. It's a color heuristic, not real image recognition, so always double-check the rating before saving.

Rating either one feeds real calibration: once you've rated a few shoots, the app starts correcting future predictions toward what you actually saw at your locations.

Sound & feedback

Haptic buzz, tap sounds, a shutter-click when you press Refresh Data, and a little confetti burst when new data lands. None of this ever plays on its own in the background - only when you actually tap something. Sound is on by default; turn it off here if you'd rather have it silent in the field.

The dashboard quietly re-checks weather every 5 minutes on its own, with no sound and no popup - just numbers updating in place. Turn it off if you'd rather only ever refresh by pressing the button yourself.
