      7-Day Sunrise/Sunset Quality Outlook

      Quality = real cloud cover by altitude band + humidity at the sunrise/sunset hour, from Open-Meteo's hourly forecast. Not a photo of the sky — a physical model of whether one is likely to show color.

      Weather data comes from Open-Meteo, which blends 15+ national weather agency models (NOAA, ECMWF, DWD, and others) and picks the highest-resolution one for your spot. Another weather app can still show a slightly different number for the same hour — that's normal forecast-model variance, not an error.

      Sun Direction Finder

      Where the sun sits in the sky, right now or at any time you pick, for lining up a shot before the light arrives. Altitude/azimuth from the same solar model as the rest of the app — no map tiles, so it works offline and costs nothing to run.

      Sound & feedback

      Haptic buzz, tap sounds, a shutter-click when you press Refresh Data, and a little confetti burst when new data lands. None of this ever plays on its own in the background - only when you actually tap something. Sound is on by default; turn it off here if you'd rather have it silent in the field.

      Legendary condition alerts

      When the sunrise/sunset quality or bird activity forecast crosses into "Legendary" territory, get a real device notification - not just an in-app badge - even if the app isn't open. Needs your browser/OS notification permission once.

      Notifications

      Data

      The dashboard quietly re-checks weather every 5 minutes on its own, with no sound and no popup - just numbers updating in place. Turn it off if you'd rather only ever refresh by pressing the button yourself.

      Background auto-refresh

      If the dashboard ever looks stuck showing old numbers, this throws away the saved last-known-good reading and pulls a completely fresh one.

      Weather data comes from Open-Meteo, which blends 15+ national weather agency models (NOAA, ECMWF, DWD, and others) and picks the highest-resolution one for your spot — it's free, so this app has no ongoing data cost. Another weather app can still show a slightly different number for the same hour; that's normal forecast-model variance between two different sources, not an error in either one.
